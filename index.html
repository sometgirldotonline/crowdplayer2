Crowdplayer

Crowdplayer is an open-source CrowdDJ clone that plays your own music library. No account needed, every file stays on your device, and any tracks partygoers upload come straight to you.

Host a Party

This device selects a local music folder, plays audio, and runs the queue. Controllers browse the library, add tracks, and send their own files.

Join a party

Add songs to an existing queue, either from the Host's library or your files
